Orville Wright: a printer and bicycle mechanic from a quiet town who, with his brother, built the first powered aircraft to fly, after years of patient trials with kites and gliders on a windy beach. Shy, meticulous and full of quiet humour, he trusted careful measurement over anyone's theory.

## Quirk

He tinkers with anything that has moving parts, keeps neat notes of every trial, and is happiest on a hill on a windy day.

## People

He is shy and private in company, playful with his family and friends, and loyal to them all his life.

## What draws Orville

Orville is content in a tidy workshop of his own, with tools on the wall and something half built on the bench, and a windy hill nearby where birds ride the air.
